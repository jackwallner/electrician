## Why 2026 and 2023 can coexist

`2026` identifies the current exam-preparation shopping cycle. It does not identify the edition of the code tables in the app. Current autocomplete contains year-suffixed phrases such as `electrician exam prep 2026`, `electrician test prep 2026`, `nec electrical exam prep 2026`, and `electrician practice test 2026`. Competing titles also use the cohort year.

`2023 NEC` is the technical scope. The app's calculations and citations use the 2023 cycle. Jurisdictions adopt editions and amendments on different schedules. The listing must never collapse those two facts into `2026 NEC`, `current NEC everywhere`, or an implication that a 2026-branded app matches every 2026 exam.

Required consistency:

- The title may retain 2026 as the shopping cohort.
- The subtitle must retain 2023 NEC.
- The first description paragraph must state that the app uses 2023 NEC values and that jurisdictions vary.
- Onboarding and Settings must show the edition before a candidate relies on practice answers.
- Every calculation result must continue to identify its edition and safe article citation.
- Screenshots that show a Home or result state must leave the 2023 NEC label legible.
- The annual title suffix should be reviewed each year. Do not change it automatically without an active search cohort and a compatible exam market.

## Positioning and claim boundaries

Recommended position:

> Focused 2023 NEC navigation and calculation practice for journeyman and master electrician candidates

Defensible advantages:

- Practice finding the right article before opening the book
- Work calculations in explicit numbered steps
- See the trap behind a wrong answer
- Generate fresh problems across five supported calculation shapes
- Review authored mistakes and practice targeted variants where implemented
- Use free study calculators with stated assumptions
- Track practice activity and readiness signals limited to the app's coverage
- Practice without an account, with history stored on the device

Do not claim:

- Complete journeyman or master exam coverage
- A realistic full mock exam unless an actual blueprint-aligned mode is added
- A pass prediction, guaranteed score improvement, license outcome, or readiness certification
- Current 2026 NEC tables
- Validity in every state or jurisdiction
- Code compliance or a substitute for the code in force
- Official NFPA status, endorsement, affiliation, reproduced code text, or ownership of NEC content
- Field engineering, design approval, or safety-critical professional advice

The app is a study aid. Every technical result should remain traceable to the 2023 edition and carry assumptions where a field calculator simplifies a real installation.
