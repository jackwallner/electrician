## Notes per rollout

### Ask to Buy
- Purchase paths: `OnboardingView.primaryAction` (trial page), `PaywallView.purchase` (paywall, which is also the locked-room and Settings sheet). Both go through `SubscriptionService.purchase`. There is no separate trial sheet and no StoreKit 2 path.
- Later approval: `SubscriptionService` is the `PurchasesDelegate`, and `receivedUpdated` calls `apply`, which sets `isPro`. The paywall dismisses on `isPro` and onboarding calls `startTour()` on `isPro` while on the trial step, so approval unlocks without a relaunch. Already present, unchanged.
- No pending flag is stored, so there is nothing to clear that could fire a stale action. The onboarding notice is view state and is cleared on the next tap or on Back.
- The purchase button does not spin: both callers reset `purchasing` in a `defer`.
- The app has no analytics capture call and no purchase outcome logging, so nothing to log distinctly.
- Network errors in onboarding use `.storeUnreachable`, not `.offeringsUnavailable`: the latter opens the plan picker sheet, and a connection drop mid-purchase must not open a second paywall.

### Remote config
- Control text is today's copy character for character: `Start <trial length> free` (the store's length, "7 days") and, with no trial available, `Subscribe to Electrician+`. The paywall and onboarding are both sentence case here, so unlike Mahj there is no separate title-case surface; `my` and `your` read "Start my free trial" and "Start your free trial" on both.
- Ineligible accounts see `Subscribe to Electrician+` in every arm (3.1.2). The lifetime button is not a trial button and ignores the arm.
- A test is set by putting `trial_cta` in the offering metadata (Experiment variant metadata works because the current offering is read). Keys a switch reads (`rating_prompt`) must be identical on every offering.

### Star prompt: moments
No analytics exists in the app, so frequencies are estimates from the product's structure, not measurements.

| Moment | Trigger | Typical reach |
|---|---|---|
| Drill finished, half right or better, or an ungraded flashcard set | `DrillCompleteView` (quiz, calc, flashcard, practice run, quick session) | The core action. The free rooms are one tap from Home and a session is a handful of questions, so a typical user finishes one in session 1 and again in session 2 or 3. The first session's moment is blocked by the second-launch gate, so the ask comes at the first good finish in launch 2. |
| Code Minute finished, half right or better | `CodeMinuteResultView` | Daily five questions on Home; a user who returns reaches it in session 2 or 3. |

Both are core actions, so there is no rare-only moment and no launch, appear or open-count trigger anywhere (`recordAppLaunch` only counts launches for the gate). Not asked: onboarding, the first session, after a weak score, after a paywall or purchase sheet.

Why half: a scored drill under half is the one result where the ask reads as tone-deaf, and a typical drill ends above half. Ungraded flashcards have no bad result, so finishing counts.

Gates unchanged: second cold launch, 30 day cooldown stamped when the call is made, the Settings Rate row ends the passive asks, sending feedback never does, onboarding finished. Settings keeps its `?action=write-review` row. No enjoyment gate, no pre-prompt, never from a button.
