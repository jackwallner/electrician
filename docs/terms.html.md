## A study aid, not a code book

The app teaches concepts and calculations in original wording, carries the underlying numbers from the 2023 NEC, and cites article numbers so you can verify each one yourself. It reproduces no NFPA text and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the NFPA. Field tools are study estimates with stated assumptions. Your jurisdiction may examine against a different edition or amend it locally; the code in force where you work and the authority having jurisdiction have the final word. The app does not guarantee an exam result.
