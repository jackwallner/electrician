# Android Privacy Policy: Electrician Exam Prep

> Electrician Exam Prep for Android keeps practice data on your device. Google Play and RevenueCat handle subscriptions.

Web page: https://jackwallner.github.io/electrician/android-privacy.html

## Android Privacy Policy

Electrician Exam Prep for Android · last updated 20 October 2026

Electrician Exam Prep does not sell your personal information, show ads, or track you across apps or websites.

## What stays on your device

Your practice history, per-room accuracy, named mistakes, streaks, review queue, Code Minute results, exam target (licence, jurisdiction, code edition, exam date, focus areas) and settings are stored on your device only. They are not uploaded anywhere, and we cannot read them. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes them.

## Purchases

If you subscribe to Electrician+ or buy the lifetime unlock, the purchase is handled by Google Play and by RevenueCat, our subscription provider. They receive an app-generated anonymous identifier, the device and app version, and the details of the transaction so the app can tell whether your membership is active. They do not receive your name, email address or practice history. Google's handling of your payment is governed by the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy); RevenueCat's by the [RevenueCat Privacy Policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/).

## Network use

The app connects to the internet only to load subscription prices and verify purchases. Every calculation, question and field tool works offline.

## Analytics and tracking

There is no advertising, no third-party analytics SDK, and no cross-app tracking. The app does not request the advertising ID.

## Notifications

If you turn on a reminder, the app asks for notification permission and schedules the reminder on your device. Nothing about it is sent anywhere.

## Data you send us deliberately

Send Feedback opens an email draft addressed to us. We receive only what you choose to write and send, and use it only to answer you.

## Deleting your data

Settings > Reset Progress clears your practice history. Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on the device. To ask RevenueCat to delete the anonymous purchase record, email us and we will request it.

## Children

The app is a professional study aid and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
