Privacy Policy

Electrician Exam Practice 2026 · last updated 25 August 2026

Electrician Exam Practice 2026 does not collect, transmit, or sell your personal information.

What stays on your device

Your practice history, per-topic accuracy, streaks, review queue, daily challenge results, and settings are stored on your device only. They are not uploaded anywhere, and we cannot read them.

Purchases

If you subscribe to Electrician+, the purchase is handled by Apple and by RevenueCat, our subscription provider. They receive an anonymous identifier and the details of the transaction so the app can tell whether your subscription is active. They do not receive your name, email address, or practice history. Apple's handling of your payment is governed by Apple's own privacy policy.

Analytics and tracking

There is no advertising, no third-party analytics SDK, and no cross-app or cross-site tracking. The app does not request the advertising identifier.

Data you send us deliberately

If you use the in-app feedback option, it opens an email draft addressed to us. We receive only what you choose to write and send, and we use it only to answer you.

Children

The app is a professional study aid and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
