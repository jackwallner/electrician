## Which code edition does the app use?

Every answer uses the 2023 NEC. The app names that edition on every result
screen, in Settings, and in your Exam Target, so you always know what you
are checking against. Requirements vary by the edition your jurisdiction
has adopted and by local amendments.

## What does the app cover?

It focuses on code-book navigation, conductors and ampacity, worked
calculations, and grounding with motors. Services, feeders, load
calculations, wiring methods, and special occupancy chapters are not
covered yet.

## Restoring a purchase

Settings > Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the
same Apple Account you bought with.

## Managing or cancelling a subscription

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us: iOS Settings > your
name > Subscriptions. Cancelling there stops future renewals.

This app is a study aid, not a code book. It is not affiliated with,
endorsed by, or connected to the NFPA. It teaches concepts and
calculations in original wording and cites article numbers so you can
verify each one in the code in force where you work.
