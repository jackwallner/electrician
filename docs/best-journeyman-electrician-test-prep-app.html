The apps, one by one

NEC Electrical Exam by Dakota

The most-rated exam prep app in this group. It lists over 3,000 questions across six exam sections, walkthroughs with NEC references, practice by topic, study stats and an AI tutor for step-by-step help. Its listing names the exams it targets state by state, from journeyman and master to contractor licenses. The trade-off: the listing does not name the NEC edition its questions follow, and the AI tutor and full access are paid.

NEC Mastery

NEC Mastery says it is built on the 2023 NEC with 2024 updates and lists 8,000+ questions with explanations, in English and Spanish. Beyond practice it adds 20 calculators, an offline quick reference for ampacity, conduit fill and motor tables, and a work-hour log with PDF export. The trade-off: the full question bank is a paid purchase, and with this much in one app there is a lot to find your way around.

Journeyman Electrician Pro

Built around exam simulation: an 80-question, 240-minute timed exam, a 40-question quick exam, five 60-question practice tests, a 25-question diagnostic and a passing-probability gauge. The free tier includes selected subtopics, three practice tests, daily challenges, flashcards and the diagnostic. The trade-offs: its listing says hundreds of questions rather than thousands, it does not name an NEC edition, and the upgrade is subscription only, including a weekly plan.

Journeyman Electrician (2025)

A straightforward question bank with practice by category, mock tests, test history and bookmarks. It is free, with a purchase to remove ads. The trade-off: it was last updated on January 16, 2025, and the listing does not say which NEC edition the questions use, so check answers against your book.

Electrician Exam Practice 2026 (my app)

This app drills the parts of the exam that cost time: finding the right article fast, and the calculations (ampacity derating, overcurrent sizing, conduit and box fill, voltage drop, grounding conductors, motors and the dwelling service calculation). Calculation problems are generated, so they do not repeat, every wrong choice matches one common mistake, and a miss shows numbered steps. It uses 2023 NEC values and asks for your state to show which edition it examines on. Three rooms and eight field calculators are free. Its limits: it is not a full exam blueprint (special occupancies, hazardous locations and low-voltage chapters are not covered), it has a 90-second timed challenge instead of a full mock exam, and it has no ratings yet.

Which should you pick?

  • You want questions aimed at your state's named exam: NEC Electrical Exam by Dakota.
  • You want the biggest bank, Spanish, or calculators you will keep using on the job: NEC Mastery.
  • You want to sit full timed practice exams before test day: Journeyman Electrician Pro.
  • You only want a free question bank to flip through: Journeyman Electrician (2025), checked against your code book.
  • You keep losing points on derating, fill and load calculations: Electrician Exam Practice 2026.
  • Many candidates pair a question bank with a calculation drill. Nothing stops you from using two.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best free journeyman electrician practice app?

All five are free to download. Journeyman Electrician Pro gives three practice tests, daily challenges, flashcards and a diagnostic free. Electrician Exam Practice 2026 keeps three practice rooms and eight field calculators free. Dakota offers free practice questions, and Journeyman Electrician (2025) is free with a purchase to remove ads.

Which NEC edition do these apps use?

NEC Mastery says it is built on the 2023 NEC with 2024 updates, and Electrician Exam Practice 2026 uses 2023 NEC values. The Dakota, Journeyman Electrician Pro and Journeyman Electrician (2025) listings do not name an edition. Check which edition your state examines on before you trust any number.

Which app has full timed practice exams?

Journeyman Electrician Pro lists an 80-question, 240-minute timed exam and a 40-question quick exam. NEC Mastery and Journeyman Electrician (2025) list mock tests. Electrician Exam Practice 2026 has a 90-second timed challenge rather than a full mock exam.

Can a practice app replace the NEC code book?

No. Exams are written against the code book your state adopted, and you need to find answers in it quickly. Electrician Exam Practice 2026 cites article numbers instead of reproducing code text, so you still need the book.
