The electrician licensing exam is open book. That is not the relief it sounds like. It is timed, and every question you have to hunt for costs you two more you never reach. Electrician is a focused practice companion for apprentice, residential, journeyman, master and contractor candidates working from 2023 NEC values.

This is not a complete exam blueprint. It drills the code navigation and calculations this app actually covers, and tells you what it does not cover so you can build the rest of your study plan elsewhere.

FIND IT FAST
Read a scenario and name the article before you open anything. Article 310 or Article 240. Chapter 9 or the raceway article. The navigation drill is the one nobody builds and the one that saves you minutes on exam day.

THE CALCULATIONS THAT FAIL PEOPLE
Ampacity derating. Overcurrent sizing. Conduit fill. Box fill. Voltage drop. Equipment and electrode grounding conductors. Motor conductors and motor protection. The full dwelling service calculation. Every one is a pure calculation with exactly one right answer, which means we generate them instead of shipping the same fifty questions in a new order. Practice as long as you want and never see a repeat.

WRONG ANSWERS THAT TEACH
Every incorrect choice is the number you get from one specific, common mistake: starting the derate in the 75 degree column, missing the small-conductor cap in 240.4(D), using 53 percent fill out of habit, counting each ground wire separately. Pick one and you find out which mistake you made, not just that you missed.

SHOW THE WORKING
Miss a calculation and you get numbered steps, not a paragraph. Almost nobody fails these on arithmetic. They fail on one skipped step, and a paragraph hides which one.

BUILT AROUND YOUR MISTAKES
Progress tracks accuracy by room and by calculation, and names the specific errors that keep catching you. Fix My Mistakes brings back authored questions you still need to review, while generated practice builds new problems that set the same trap again. Exam Warm-Up builds a short session from your weakest area.

WHAT IS COVERED
Six rooms: how the code book is organized and how to find an article, conductors and ampacity, the installation rules you look up on site, worked calculations, dwelling service and load calculations, and grounding with motors. Plus ten generated calculation shapes that never run out. Special occupancies, hazardous locations and the low-voltage chapters are not covered yet.

A NOTE ON THE CODE
Every answer in this app is built on the 2023 NEC, and every table it computes from is unchanged across the 2020 and 2023 cycles, so the arithmetic is the same arithmetic your paper asks for. What does move between editions is coverage: GFCI and AFCI scope, surge protection, equipment disconnects. Those are taught here as rules to confirm in your own book rather than as numbers to memorise, and the app has a screen that lists exactly which tables are stable and which areas are not. The edition is shown on every result so you always know what you are checking against.

This app is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the NFPA. It teaches concepts and calculations in original wording and cites article numbers so you can look each one up in your own code book. It does not reproduce code text, and it is a study aid rather than a substitute for the code book itself. Always verify against the code in force where you work.
