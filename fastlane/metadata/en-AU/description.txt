UNITED STATES ELECTRICAL CODE ONLY
Every value in this app comes from the 2023 National Electrical Code, the standard used in the United States. It is not a study aid for any other country's wiring rules, and the app is in English.

The US electrician licensing exam is open book. That is not the relief it sounds like. It is timed, and every question you have to hunt for costs you two more you never reach.

FIND IT FAST
Read a scenario and name the article before you open anything. Article 310 or Article 240. This is the drill that saves minutes on exam day.

THE CALCULATIONS THAT FAIL PEOPLE
Ampacity derating, overcurrent sizing, conduit fill, box fill, voltage drop, grounding conductors, motor circuits and the dwelling service calculation. Each is generated rather than repeated from a fixed bank, so you can practise as long as you want and never see the same problem twice.

WRONG ANSWERS THAT TEACH
Every incorrect choice is the number you get from one specific, common mistake. Pick one and you learn which mistake you made, not just that you missed.

SHOW THE WORKING
Miss a calculation and you get numbered steps, not a paragraph. Almost nobody fails these on arithmetic. They fail on one skipped step.
